# PayPal AI Hackathon 2026 rules audit

Verified 2026-10-09 against the official Devpost rules.

- Submission period ends 2026-11-12 at 12:00 PM PST (approximately 22:00 Europe/Kyiv).
- Project must centrally integrate PayPal's free Sandbox and an AI tool/model/platform.
- Existing ideas are allowed only with significant new work during the submission period; this repository contains a specific disclosure.
- Functional access may be a hosted demo or complete repository setup/run instructions.
- Public GitHub, GitLab, or Bitbucket repository and visible open-source license are mandatory.
- Public YouTube demonstration video under three minutes is mandatory.
- Materials and testing instructions must be in English.
- Judge access must remain free through judging.
- Primary eligible category: Best Use of Agentic Commerce ($5,000 USD).

Official source: https://paypalaihackathon.devpost.com/rules
